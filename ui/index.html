Help

Getting started, shortcuts and keeping the bot in sync with your game.

Start fishing

Join Fisch, choose your rod and enchants on the Rods page, then press Start or F7. The shortcut uses your latest saved settings.

Press Stop or F9 to end a run or cancel a rod or quest search. Temporary run data is removed unless Keep logs is on.

Every run calibrates for your current window and area. Hover any (?) for details about a setting.

Saved setups & shortcuts

Create setups on the Dashboard with + New setup (Quick switch card). Press F6 to cycle through them while fishing; a switch waits for the current cast to finish.

Enable Equip rod in game to switch the rod through the Equipment Bag. Totem and bait settings stay separate from rod setups.

Change shortcuts in Settings. Use a key name or combination, such as f7 or ctrl+alt+r. Each action needs a different shortcut.

Rod checks & window sizes

At start, then every two minutes at the next cast boundary, the bot checks the rod name and held frame in your hotbar. If it can identify the rod but it is not held, it presses that slot once and verifies the result.

Unreadable labels cause a logged skip, without guessing a key. Keep the rod visible in the hotbar. Checks and bag actions wait until the rod is reeled in.

The bot follows the window's position and size between casts. After a resize, it relearns reel geometry. Let a fish finish before resizing, and keep the window steady during bag actions.

Community & feedback

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome. For a bug, turn on Keep logs (Settings), reproduce it, then attach that run's bot.log -- it holds numbers and small reel-bar crops only, never full screenshots.
